Smart agriculture helps farmers make better decisions with live data.
Soil moisture sensors tell farmers when crops need water.
Weather stations report rainfall, temperature and humidity every hour.
An IoT gateway on the farm forwards readings to a mobile dashboard.
Farmers in Rwanda use these dashboards to plan irrigation and harvests.
Drones capture images that show which parts of a field are stressed.
Combining drone images with IoT sensor data improves crop yields.
Low-power radio networks let sensors run for years on small batteries.
