The Internet of Things (IoT) connects everyday devices to the internet.
Sensors collect data and send it to cloud services for processing.
IoT devices include smart thermostats, wearables and security cameras.
Each device needs a network connection and a way to identify itself.

Security is one of the biggest challenges for IoT systems.
Weak passwords and outdated firmware leave many devices exposed.
Manufacturers should provide regular updates for every iot product.
Users should change default credentials as soon as a device is installed.

Edge computing moves some processing closer to the devices.
This reduces delay and saves bandwidth for large IoT deployments.
In summary, IoT brings convenience but needs careful design. IoT IoT.
