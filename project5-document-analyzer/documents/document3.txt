Smart cities use technology to improve public services.
Traffic lights adjust their timing based on the number of waiting cars.
Waste bins report when they are full so collection routes can change.
Street lights dim when nobody is around and brighten when people pass.
Air quality monitors warn residents when pollution levels rise.
City planners study the data to decide where to build new roads.
Citizens can report problems such as broken pipes through mobile apps.
Open data portals let researchers and students study how the city works.
Good governance and privacy rules are needed for these systems to succeed.
